Haemophilus influenzae type b IgG Antibody Determination 
 
Haemophilus influenzae type b IgG antibody determination by flow analysis assay bead assay. 
 
 
General information 
 
Collection container (including preservatives): Clotted blood sample tube (no preservative)   
 
Specimen type: Clotted Blood/serum 
 
Specimen transport: Ambient temperature via porter, courier, Royal Mail or DX compliant with IATA packing 
instruction 650 
 
Type and volume of sample: Clotted Blood/serum; Minimum volume 0.1mL 
 
Special precautions: If processing is delayed, refrigeration is preferable to storage at ambient temperature. 
Delays of over 48hr are undesirable.    
 
 
Laboratory information 
 
Measurement units: µg/mL 
 
Biological reference units: Not applicable 
 
Turnaround time: 28 Working Days 
 
 
Clinical information 
 
Clinical decision points: IgG of ≥ 0.15 µg/mL for short term protection:  IgG of ≥ 1.00 µg/mL for long term 
protection 
 
Factors known to significantly affect the results: None known
