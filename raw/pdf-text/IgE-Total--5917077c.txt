Turnaround time (calendar days from sample receipt to authorised result): Median - 3 
Clinical information 
Indications for the test: Patients with suspected allergy (atopic disease). Main use of the test is to help 
interpret specific IgE levels. 
 
Factors affecting the test: Total IgE >3000kU/L increases the incidence of false positive (low level) specific 
IgE. IgE <30 kU/L associated with false negative specific IgE. Similarly, very low IgE levels (<2.0 kU/L) may 
indicate an underlying immunodeficiency; measurement of immunoglobulins is recommended.
