Lyme IgG, IgM 
 
General information 
 
Collection container (including preservatives):  
6mL clotted blood tube 
CE marked leak proof container 
 
Specimen type: Venous Blood (sera or plasma); CSF is also accepted - this will be sent to the referral 
laboratory  
 
Collection: 6mL blood tube 
 
Specimen transport: Ambient temperature via porter, courier, Royal Mail or DX compliant with IATA packing 
instruction 650. 
 
Send to lab immediately for same day testing, delays are not desirable. If not sent immediately or transport 
delay expected - store at 2-8°C, testing must be done within 6 days. 
 
Minimum volume of sample:  2 mL 
 
Special precautions: If processing is delayed, refrigeration is preferable to storage at ambient temperature. 
Delays of over 48hr are undesirable.   
 
Laboratory information 
 
Measurement units: Index 
 
Turnaround time for provisional result (working days): 3 days  
Turnaround time to final result (working days): 4 days 
 
Clinical information 
 
Clinical decision points: Not applicable 
 
Factors known to significantly affect the results: Haemolysis
