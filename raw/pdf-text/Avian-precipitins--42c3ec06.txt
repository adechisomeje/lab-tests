Avian precipitins 
 
General information 
 
These tests detect the presence of IgG antibodies to pigeon and budgerigar. 
 
Specimen transport: At room temperature 
 
Repeat frequency: On appearance of new symptoms 
 
Special precautions: None 
 
Laboratory information 
 
Normal reference range: Pigeon 0 - 38 mg/L, Budgie 0 - 8 mg/L 
 
Volume and sample type: 4ml serum 
 
Method: FEIA (Fluoroenzyme immunoassay) 
 
Turnaround time (calendar days from sample receipt to authorised result): Median – 4 
 
Participation in EQA Scheme: UK NEQAS for FUNGAL and RELATED ANTIGENS 
 
Clinical information 
 
Indications for the test: Respiratory symptoms associated with avian exposure 
 
Factors affecting the test: Positive results are seen in some healthy individuals exposed to birds.
