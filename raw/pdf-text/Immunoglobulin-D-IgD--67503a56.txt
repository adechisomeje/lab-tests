General information 
 
Measurement of serum IgD can aid in the diagnosis of Hyper IgD Syndrome (HIDS) and IgD myeloma. 
 
Specimen transport: At room temperature 
 
Repeat frequency: At significant change of clinical symptoms 
 
Special precautions: IgD has a relatively short half life.  
Samples should arrive in the lab within 24 hours or stored at 4°C if there is a delay in transport. 
 
Laboratory information 
 
Normal reference range: 2-100 KU/L 
 
Volume and sample type: 4ml serum 
 
Method: ELISA 
 
Turnaround time (calendar days from sample receipt to authorised result): Median - 16, 95th percentile - 
34 
 
Clinical information 
 
Indications for the test: Familial Mediterranean Fever, underlying immunodeficiency. 
 
Factors affecting the test: None
