Anti Myelin oligodendrocyte glycoprotein (MOG) Antibodies 
(Referred Test) 
 
General information 
 
MOG antibodies have been reported in some people who have transverse myelitis and/or optic neuritis 
and/or acute disseminated encephalomyelitis and Aquaporin 4 antibody negative neuromyelitis optica. 
 
MOG antibodies are found on the myelin sheath rather than the astrocyte as in Aquaporin 4 antibodies and 
cause damage to the myelin.
