General information 
 
Specimen transport: At room temperature

Volume and sample type: 4ml serum 
 
Method: RIA

34 
 
Clinical information 
 
Indications for the test: Patients suspected of Myasthenia gravis and who are acetylcholine receptor 
antibody negative. 
 
Factors affecting the test: None
