Streptococcus pneumoniae IgG antibody determination by flow

General information  
 
Collection container (including preservatives): Clotted blood sample tube (no preservative) 
 
Specimen type: Clotted blood sample tube (no preservative) 
 
Specimen transport: At ambient temperature via porter, courier, Royal Mail or DX compliant with IATA 
packing instruction 650 
 
Minimum volume of sample: Clotted Blood/serum, paired sera; Minimum volume 0.1mL 
 
Special precautions: If processing is delayed, refrigeration is preferable to storage at ambient temperature. 
Delays of over 48hr are undesirable 
 
Laboratory information 
 
Measurement units: μg/mL 
 
Biological reference units: Not applicable 
 
Turnaround time: 28 Working Days 
 
Clinical information 
 
Clinical decision points: ≥ 0.35 μg/mL; putative correlate of protection 
 
Factors known to significantly affect the results: None known
